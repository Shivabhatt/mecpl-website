---
name: Documentary photo retouching
description: Preserve genuine MECPL photographs when removing small camera stamps or other overlays.
---

For small retouches on genuine team and site photos, preserve the original full frame and pixels outside the affected area. Use a square detail crop for the image editor, then composite only the retouched area back with a soft edge.

**Why:** Full-frame image edits returned square outputs from landscape sources and changed the framing. Local square-crop edits preserved the relevant geometry and allowed clean camera-stamp removal without regenerating people or the wider scene.

**How to apply:** Compare the cleaned corner and the full photo before connecting the derivative to the website. Keep the original photo available and preserve its dimensions in the final derivative.
