Use the exact NSCI logo shown on the Awards page for NSCI marks on About and Completed Projects pages.

**Why:** The user repeated that the Completed Projects NSCI mark must match the Awards page logo.

**How to apply:** When adding or changing an NSCI award logo on another page, use the Awards page's exact image asset rather than a related award-document scan or alternate mark.
