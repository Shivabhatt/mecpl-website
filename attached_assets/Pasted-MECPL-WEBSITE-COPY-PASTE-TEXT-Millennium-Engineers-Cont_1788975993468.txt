WHO WE ARE
========================================

Who We Are

From A ₹2 Lakh
Beginning To ₹780 Cr

Millennium Engineers & Contractors began in 1973 as a small partnership, taken on by an engineer who wasn't content working for someone else. Four and a half decades on, that same commitment to quality and timely delivery has grown MECPL into one of Pune's most trusted structural engineering and construction names — ISO-certified, CRISIL-rated, and built on 3,000+ skilled hands.

“Bringing positive changes in the lives of the people around me is the biggest achievement I've had in my life.”

Mr. M.B. Nambiar — Founder & Chairman
